🏐 A Beginner's Guide to Volleyball





Volleyball is a team sport in which two teams—usually of six players each indoors or two players per side in beach variants—hit a large inflated ball back and forth over a high net using their hands and arms without letting it touch the ground. Points are scored when the ball lands on the opponent's floor or when the opposing team commits a fault.

📒 Basic Rules

  • No Double Contacts
  • In-Bounds and Out-of-Bounds
  • Rotation and Scoring
